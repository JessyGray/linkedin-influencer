# 04 · Content

## Pillars

Choose 3 or 4 pillars where three things overlap: what the person really does (`facts.md`), what the audience wants, and a gap from `market.md`. Rotate them so no pillar appears twice in a row.

Common pillars: what I built, what I learned, lessons from mistakes, opinions on the field, behind the scenes, helping others (how-to).

## Formats

| Format | Shape | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story | Moment → problem → what I did → what I learned | Trust, reach |
| Lesson | Claim → 3 to 5 short points → takeaway | Saves, follows |
| How-to | Problem → steps → result | Saves |
| Build log | What I shipped this week, one detail, what is next | Proof of work |
| Opinion | Clear stance → why → where I could be wrong | Comments |

The publisher posts text only. Plan images, documents and polls as `"manual": true` items the person posts by hand.

## Writing rules

- **Hook:** the first 2 lines decide everything. Make them specific, under 200 characters together, no greeting.
- **Length:** 600 to 1,300 characters for most posts. Short lines, one idea per paragraph.
- **One idea per post.** If there are two, make two posts.
- **End:** a real question the person wants answered, or nothing. No "Thoughts?" on every post.
- **Hashtags:** 0 to 3, at the end.
- **Links:** keep them out of the body unless the post is about the link.
- **Language:** the one in `facts.md`. Keep it at the level the person actually writes. Simple beats polished.

## Sound like a person

Many readers can spot AI text now. Avoid:

- Em dashes (—). Use a period or a comma.
- Words: delve, leverage, unlock, game-changer, landscape, journey (as a metaphor), elevate, seamless, robust, testament, tapestry, navigate, foster, realm, embark, "in today's fast-paced world", "here's the thing".
- Lists of exactly three adjectives. "Not X, but Y" in every post. Every line as its own paragraph.
- Emoji bullets. Bold "Key takeaway:" labels.

Do: use details only this person knows (a place, a tool, a time of day, what someone said). Vary sentence length. Admit what went wrong.

## Self-check (run on every post before it goes into `schedule.json`)

1. Every fact is in `facts.md`. No made-up numbers, names, clients or results.
2. No `[ASK]` left in the text.
3. Hook under 200 characters, whole post under 3,000.
4. None of the banned words or patterns above.
5. Would the person say this out loud to a colleague? If not, rewrite it.

A post that fails gets fixed or dropped. It never goes out as it is.
