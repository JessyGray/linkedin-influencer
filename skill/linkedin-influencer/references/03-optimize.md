# 03 · Optimize

Write copy the person can paste as is. Use only `facts.md`. Use keywords from `market.md`.

## Limits

| Field | Max characters |
| --- | --- |
| Headline | 220 |
| About | 2,600 |
| Experience description | 2,000 |
| Post | 3,000 |

## Headline

Pick one formula and fill it with real facts:

- `<Role> · <what they build or do> for <who> · <proof>`
- `<Role> at <company> | <specialty> | <current project>`
- `<Target role> in training · <stack or field> · building <real project>`

Write 3 options. Recommend one and say why in one line.

## About (4 short blocks)

1. **Hook:** 1 to 2 lines that show who they are and why it matters to the reader. This is the part people see before "see more".
2. **What I do:** plain sentences. Name the tools, the audience and the kind of work.
3. **Proof:** 2 to 4 real examples. Numbers only if they are in `facts.md`.
4. **Next step:** what to contact them about and how.

First person. Short paragraphs. No "passionate", "results-driven", "dynamic", "synergy".

## Experience

For each role: 2 to 4 bullets. Pattern: verb + what + result or scope. If there is no measured result, describe the scope honestly ("for a 12-table restaurant", "used by 3 staff").

## Also deliver

- Top 5 skills, in order.
- Banner text, under 8 words, plus the URL or handle to show.
- Featured: which 1 to 3 items to pin.
- Custom URL suggestion.

## Output: `profile.md`

One section per field, each in a code block for easy copying, followed by a checklist of where to paste it.
