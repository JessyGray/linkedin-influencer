# 02 · Competitors

Goal: learn what already works for people the audience follows, then find the space nobody owns.

## Who to pick (5 people)

- **2 leaders:** well known in the field and market, posting every week.
- **3 peers:** one or two steps ahead of the person (same role, same country or language, 1k to 20k followers). These teach more than famous accounts because their tactics are copyable.

## How to search (max 8 searches)

Use public web search only. Good queries:

- `site:linkedin.com/in "<target role>" "<city or country>"`
- `site:linkedin.com/posts "<target role>" <topic>`
- `top LinkedIn creators <field> <country> <year>`

If the person already knows people they admire, start with those and skip searching.

## What to record for each

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Headline formula | "Role · who they help · proof" |
| About opening | First sentence, word for word |
| Content pillars | 3 to 4 topics they repeat |
| Formats | Story, lesson, how-to, opinion, build log, document, poll |
| Rhythm | Posts per week, days they post |
| Best hook seen | One first line that clearly worked (high reactions in the snippet) |

Do not copy anyone's text into the person's posts. Record patterns, not paragraphs.

## Output: `market.md`

```
# Market · <field> · <country> · <date>

| Person | Followers | Headline formula | Pillars | Formats | Rhythm |
...

## Patterns that work here
- ...

## Gaps the person can own
- Topics or angles the audience wants that nobody covers well, and that fit facts.md.

## Keywords to use
- Words that appear in most headlines and job posts for the target role.
```
