Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This application is a self-hosted copy of the open-source LinkedIn Influencer publisher (made by Jessy Gray). The person who deployed it (the "owner") uses it to publish their own posts to their own LinkedIn profile on a schedule. It is not offered to other users.

What data we access

When the owner connects LinkedIn, the app receives the owner's LinkedIn member ID, name and an access token, using the scopes openid, profile and w_member_social.

How we use it

The access token is used only to publish the owner's scheduled posts. The app does not read feeds, messages, connections or any data about other LinkedIn members.

Storage and security

The token and the post queue are stored in a private database protected by row-level security. Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

The token expires after 60 days. Access can be revoked at any time in LinkedIn settings under Data privacy, Permitted services, which immediately stops the app from posting.
